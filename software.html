GLOW and GLoW

GLOW, all capitals, is this ERC project. GLoW, with a lowercase “o”, is the wave-optics lensing code. The code predates the grant and has its own authors and its own citation. The project extends it. The distinction matters when citing either one, and it is why the code's page is at glow-code.html: a URL cannot carry a lowercase letter as a load-bearing difference.
