Gravitational Lensing of Waves

An ERC Consolidator Grant project · {{ site.project.start }}–{{ site.project.end }}

Gravitational waves and fast radio bursts are coherent: their phase survives the journey across the Universe. A lens in the way does not simply magnify them, it distorts their wavefront, leaving a frequency-dependent imprint of the matter it is made of. GLOW builds the theory, the methods and the software to read that imprint — and uses it to find lensed events, to weigh dark matter on scales nothing else reaches, and to test how gravity propagates.

The scientific case Work packages Join the project

What the project sets out to do

  • Discover lensed gravitational waves Including sources at high redshift and near supermassive black holes
  • Probe dark-matter objects, gravity and dark energy Using diffraction as a probe of structure on sub-galactic scales
  • Be ready for what is coming Upcoming fast-radio-burst surveys, next-generation detectors, LISA, and multi-messenger follow-up
