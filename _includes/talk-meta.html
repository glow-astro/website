{%- comment -%} Everything about a talk except its title, in the one order the owner set on 2026-08-28: event, institution, city, country, speakers. Shared by both talk lists so the two cannot drift apart. Middots separate the groups; commas join within one. City and country are a place, not two facts ("Madrid, Spain"), and speakers are a list ("Choi, Urrutia, Zumalacárregui"), so both read as commas while the boundaries between event, place and people stay visible. Speakers are listed alphabetically by surname in the data file, all of them in the same list.
