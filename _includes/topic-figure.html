{%- comment -%} The animation at the head of a topic page. Pass a topic from _data/topics.yml: {% include topic-figure.html topic=t %} Autoplay needs `muted` -- browsers block autoplay of anything that could make noise -- and `playsinline` stops iOS Safari hijacking it into fullscreen. The poster is what a reader sees while the video loads, and what they keep if it never does, so it is a real frame rather than a placeholder. There are no controls by default: these are short seamless loops, not clips anyone needs to scrub. Readers who ask for reduced motion get controls and a paused first frame instead -- see assets/js/reduced-motion.js. `aria-label` describes the motion for someone who cannot see it; the caption below says what it means. The two are different jobs and neither replaces the other. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign t = include.topic -%}
