{%- comment -%} The animation at the head of a topic page. Pass a topic from _data/topics.yml: {% include topic-figure.html topic=t %} Autoplay needs `muted` -- browsers block autoplay of anything that could make noise -- and `playsinline` stops iOS Safari hijacking it into fullscreen. The poster is what a reader sees while the video loads, and what they keep if it never does, so it is a real frame rather than a placeholder. There are no controls by default: these are short seamless loops, not clips anyone needs to scrub. Readers who ask for reduced motion get controls and a paused first frame instead -- see assets/js/reduced-motion.js. `aria-label` describes the motion for someone who cannot see it; the caption below says what it means. The two are different jobs and neither replaces the other. `media` is optional in _data/topics.yml -- not every topic has a render -- so this renders nothing rather than emitting /media/.webm. A stale `topic_id` once produced exactly that silently, which is why the check is here and not only in the caller. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign t = include.topic -%} {%- if t.media -%} {%- comment -%} The file is named after what it SHOWS, via `media.file`, falling back to the topic id. Not cosmetic: reusing one name for two different renders means every browser that has been here before serves the old video out of cache, which looks exactly like a template bug and cost one round of confusion already. A new animation gets a new name, and the stale one expires on its own. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign stem = t.media.file | default: t.id -%} {%- endif -%}
