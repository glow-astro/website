{%- comment -%} The source-lens-observer diagram: the science overview, built entirely from _data/topics.yml so it cannot drift from the topic bar or the topic pages. The image is a frame from the GLOW outreach video -- a binary, a lens galaxy and the Earth, with the wave's path drawn across it. Everything else is HTML cards laid over it, so relabelling a topic is a data edit and never an image edit. Two axes, and they mean different things: top row what the project builds. Order is left to right, no more. bottom row where the physics acts. The three columns line up with the source, the lens and the observer in the picture beneath, so a card's position is a location on the light path -- not a rank, and not a category. That second point is why this figure may group when _includes/topicnav.html may not: the bar would have to claim the topics sort into kinds, and they do not. Placing microlensing and dark matter both at the lens shows the overlap rather than hiding it. See the header of _data/topics.yml. Below 940px the cards drop out of the picture and stack underneath it, so the positional argument is lost on a phone. The figcaption carries it in words for exactly that reason -- and for anyone reading without the image. {%- endcomment -%}
