{%- comment -%} The Software & data submenu: every page in the section, on the overview and on each package page. A page marks itself current by setting `package_id` in its front matter; the overview sets none, so Overview is marked there instead. Driven by `section` in _data/software.yml, which also draws the cards on the overview, so the bar and the map cannot disagree about what the section holds. Deliberately a sibling of topicnav.html rather than a parameter of it. The two render the same component -- same `.topicnav` class, same styles, same measurement in assets/js/sticky-bars.js, which queries `.topicnav` and not the science page -- but they answer different questions about what is current, and the thirty lines of reasoning in topicnav.html are all about the science topics. Merging them would mean a shared include carrying both sets of comments and neither page's argument. If a third section ever wants a bar, that is the moment to parameterise, not now. The `aria-label` differs from the science bar's on purpose: a screen-reader user listing the landmarks on a page hears which section's bar this is. {%- endcomment -%}
