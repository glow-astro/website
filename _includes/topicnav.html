The topics overlap -- microlensing is both a wave-optics problem and a dark-matter probe -- so headings that sort them into categories claim a structure the science does not have. See _data/topics.yml. This replaces the per-page section bar on topic pages rather than sitting above it. Three levels of navigation on a short page is more chrome than content, and which topic you are in matters more than which paragraph. Plain links, no JavaScript, no disclosure widget: six items fit on one line, so hiding them behind a control would cost a click and gain nothing. {%- endcomment -%}
