{%- comment -%} The science submenu: every topic, grouped, on the hub and on each topic page. A page marks itself current by setting `topic_id` in its front matter; the hub sets none, so nothing is marked there. This replaces the per-page section bar on topic pages rather than sitting above it. Three levels of navigation on one short page is more chrome than content, and which topic you are in matters more than which paragraph. Plain links, no JavaScript, no disclosure widget: the whole list is six items and fits on one line, so hiding it behind a control would cost a click and gain nothing. {%- endcomment -%}
