{%- comment -%} The "See also" block at the foot of every topic page: the work-package tasks the topic feeds, and the papers behind it. Restructured 2026-09-07, at the owner's word. It used to be a two-column grid, one cell for the work packages and one for the papers, each a run of links separated by middots. That reads at two papers and fails at twenty: the tasks are capped at three by the programme and will stay so, the papers are not capped by anything, so one cell grows without bound beside one that never does. The two are now stacked bands of equal width, and the papers are a list, one per line, so the block grows downward instead of sideways. The list is capped at six, newest first, with the remainder pointing at the publications page. The cap is the point: a topic page should say what the work rests on, not become a second copy of the publications list. Order comes from `date` in _data/publications.yml, not from the order of the topic's own `papers` list, so a hand-written order cannot go stale. The ids are still the topic's, and an id with no matching paper is caught by the data-integrity check in docs/SITE_CONVENTIONS.md §3. This include carries the whole block, markup included, because the six topic pages held an identical copy of it until today and identical copies drift. Takes: include.topic (a _data/topics.yml entry). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign total = include.topic.papers | size -%} {%- assign limit = 6 -%}
