{%- comment -%} One person card, used by every group on team.html: the PI, postdocs, doctoral and Master's students. Until 2026-08-28 this markup was repeated four times in the page, on the reasoning that eleven lines did not justify an include. Adding the avatar took it past that: four copies of a photo-or- initials fallback is four places for the fallback to be got wrong. The avatar is a photograph when `photo` is set and the person's initials when it is not. Initials are a fallback, not a placeholder to be filled in later: nobody has to supply a photograph, and a card without one is finished. Initials are the first letter of the first and last words of the name, which is the same rule the personal site uses. It is wrong for some names and right for most; a person who wants different initials should get an explicit field rather than a cleverer rule. `bio` is the PI's longer line and `topic` everyone else's. A card renders whichever it has. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign p = include.person -%}
