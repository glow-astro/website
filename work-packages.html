Data and collaboration

The methods are demonstrated on publicly available catalogs — those released by LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA and those produced by independent groups — so that the analyses can be reproduced from outside the project.

GLOW does not join the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA collaboration. An independent analysis strengthens the credibility of a lensing discovery, and staying outside preserves the freedom to question conventional assumptions. The project does collaborate with LVK on validating its software tools, in particular the DINGO-based inference intended for low-latency alerts.
